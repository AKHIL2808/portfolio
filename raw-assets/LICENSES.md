# Third-party asset licenses

Every third-party asset (model, texture, HDRI, font, sound, music) needs a row here **before** it is used anywhere in `blender/`, `raw-assets/` or `public/`.

- Only CC0 or royalty-free licenses (docs/tdd.md › Art sourcing). Good CC0 sources: Poly Haven, Kenney, Freesound (filtered by CC0).
- No copied anime characters, crests, music or voice clips.
- Original work made for this project does not need a row.
